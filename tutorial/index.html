🏎️ Initialize Your Racing Team

Every great F1 career starts at the drawing board. You need to register with the League first.

/start

Use the `/start` command in your server's designated racing channel. The bot will initialize your profile, award you **1,000 starting credits**, and allow you to name your custom Formula 1 Constructor.

Action Details
Requirements None. Can only be run once.
Rewards Constructor registration + 1,000 credits wallet.

💡 Rookie Tip

Choose a cool team name! It will be displayed in the live commentary logs and overall standings embeds throughout the championship season.

💰 Build Your Team Economy

Formula 1 is an expensive sport. Learn how to secure daily income to fund your car development.

/daily
/work
/open [crate_type]

Keep your budget healthy by using daily login commands. `/daily` pays exactly 500 credits. `/work` lets your driver do odd-jobs around the paddock for random rewards between 100¢ and 500¢. You can also unbox Loot Crates with `/open` to find rare car parts and cash bundles.

💡 Rookie Tip

Earn passive income by hanging out in voice channels (2¢ per minute) or talking in text chat (5¢ per message, 10s cooldown). Every penny counts!

🛠️ Upgrade Parts & Train Skills

Develop your machine and train your driver's capabilities to climb the grid standings.

/upgrade [part_name]
/train [skill_name]

Car parts (like Engine, Aero, and ERS) scale from level 1 to 20. Upgrades cost credits and shave seconds off your lap times. Driver skills (Pace, Consistency, aggression, overtaking) range from 1 to 100, cost 400¢ per point, and dictate your driver's base speed and overtaking efficiency.

⚠️ Damage Warning

Aggressive pacing and crashes cause wear and damage. Visit `/repairs` and run `/repair` to fix parts, as damaged components severely degrade your speed!

⚙️ Configure Race Strategy

Before the lights go out, formulate your game plan. Tires and pacing dictate your race pace.

/strategy

Once registered in a GP with `/joinrace` (costs 500¢), configure your strategy. The `/strategy` command opens a private control board to choose starting tyres and pacing.

Tyres Pace Delta Wear Rate Best Window
🟥 Soft Fastest (-0.8s) Very High (12%/lap) 3-5 Laps
🟨 Medium Moderate (-0.3s) Normal (7%/lap) 6-10 Laps
⬜ Hard Slowest (Base) Very Low (4%/lap) 11-20 Laps

💡 Strategy Insight

Starting on Softs gives you qualifying and early race pace advantages, but requires an early pit stop. Starting on Hards allows you to run long and execute a one-stop or overcut strategy!

📱 Live In-Race Pit Commands

A race is won in the cockpit, not just on paper. Manage active races in your private DMs.

/pit

When the race begins, the bot sends you a private DM containing a live telemetry card. This telemetry updates every lap with your tyre wear. When your tyres get worn (below 80% health), you lose speed. Schedule a pit stop immediately by clicking the button in your DMs or typing `/pit`!

⛈️ Wet Weather Warning

If the weather radar shows Rain, pit immediately for 🟩 Intermediates. Running slick tyres in wet conditions incurs a 15-second penalty per lap and multiplies crash probability by 4.5x!
